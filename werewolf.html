One Night · Hidden Roles

Werewolf

Each card is assigned a role. Read your role carefully — do not reveal it to other players.

Card Role Objective
2♠ Thief At the start of the game, two extra cards are shown to you. You may take on the role of one. If both cards are werewolves, you must take the werewolf role.
Q♥ Cupid In the beginning of the game, make two players become lovers. If one dies, the other dies as well.
K♠♥♣♦ Werewolf When awoken at night, agree amongst all werewolves to eliminate a player.
3♥ Little Girl You can peek when the werewolves act. Don't get spotted — and stay alive.
Q♠ Witch You have one potion of healing and one potion of killing — use them wisely.
A♦ Fortune Teller When awoken at night, you may learn whether a chosen player is a werewolf or not.
J♠ Piper When awoken at night, hypnotise 2 players. If all players are charmed or dead, you win. You are neither townfolk nor werewolf.
A♠ Hunter If you die — at daytime or night — you may kill one player.
J♦ Jester Get voted out to win.
6–9 Villager Survive.
