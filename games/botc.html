Hidden Roles · Trouble Brewing

Blood on the
Clocktower

Read your role carefully — the Storyteller wakes you when marked with an asterisk (*).

Card Role Team Ability
Demon
A♠ Imp Demon Each night*, choose a player: they die. If you kill yourself this way, a Minion becomes the Imp.
Minions
K♠ Poisoner Minion Each night*, choose a player: they are poisoned tonight and tomorrow day.
K♥ Spy Minion Each night*, you see the Grimoire. You might register as good & as a Townsfolk or Outsider, even if dead.
K♣ Baron Minion There are extra Outsiders in play. [+2 Outsiders]
K♦ Scarlet Woman Minion If there are 5 or more players alive & the Demon dies, you become the Demon. (Travellers don't count.)
Outsiders
J♠ Butler Outsider Each night*, choose a player (not yourself): tomorrow, you may only vote if they are voting too.
J♥ Saint Outsider If you die by execution, your team loses.
J♣ Recluse Outsider You might register as evil & as a Minion or Demon, even if dead.
J♦ Drunk Outsider You do not know you are the Drunk. You think you are a Townsfolk character, but you are not.
Townsfolk
2♦ Washerwoman Townsfolk You start* knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular Townsfolk.
3♦ Librarian Townsfolk You start* knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular Outsider. (Or that zero are in play.)
4♦ Investigator Townsfolk You start* knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular Minion.
5♦ Chef Townsfolk You start* knowing how many pairs of evil players there are.
6♦ Empath Townsfolk Each night*, you learn how many of your 2 alive neighbours are evil.
7♦ Fortune Teller Townsfolk Each night*, choose 2 players: you learn if either is a Demon. There is a good player that registers as a Demon to you.
8♦ Undertaker Townsfolk Each night*, you learn which character died by execution today.
9♦ Monk Townsfolk Each night*, choose a player (not yourself): they are safe from the Demon tonight.
10♦ Ravenkeeper Townsfolk If you die at night*, you are woken to choose a player: you learn their character.
Q♠ Virgin Townsfolk The 1st time you are nominated, if the nominator is a Townsfolk, they are executed immediately.
Q♥ Slayer Townsfolk Once per game, during the day*, publicly choose a player: if they are the Demon, they die.
Q♣ Soldier Townsfolk You are safe from the Demon.
Q♦ Mayor Townsfolk If only 3 players live & no execution occurs, your team wins. If you die at night, another player might die instead.
